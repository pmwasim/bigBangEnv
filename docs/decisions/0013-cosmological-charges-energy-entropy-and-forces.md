# ADR-0013: Cosmological dimensions, conserved charges, energy tensors, and the four forces

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-28

## Context

In the Genesis Specification (§1 & §5), `bigBangEnv` maps cosmological physics onto systems runtime primitives. Through ADR-0012, the runtime implemented discrete ticks, directed/paracrine/endocrine signals, molecules, five vital organs, and circadian body modes—yet every signal still carried only one authority scalar (`Trust`) and every cell drew from one flat energy counter (`spent`), with no spatial coordinates, no thermodynamic waste heat (`entropy`), no $E = mc^2$ mass condensation accounting, and no gravitational load curvature across peer Bodies.

## Decision

1. **Conserved Charge Vectors (`bigbang.physics.ChargeVector`)**:
   - Every `Signal` carries `(authority: Trust, valence: float, risk: float, affinity: str)` (with defaults preserving M0/M1 trace compatibility).
   - `risk` propagates monotonically across `Context` just like taint (`max` over consumed inputs); high-risk emissions can be capped or scrutinized without blocking low-risk work.
2. **Multi-Form Energy Tensor & Thermodynamic Entropy (`Kernel.Budget`)**:
   - Budgets track four distinct quantities:
     - `potential` (`capacity - spent`): mobilizable fuel.
     - `kinetic`: productive work (`emit`, `spend`, `step`).
     - `entropy`: waste heat generated by friction (`blocked`, `denied`, `quarantine`, `cancel`), which decays deterministically each tick (`× 0.85`, faster during `SLEEP_REM`) and amplifies `stress`.
     - `bound_mass`: condensed structural matter created when `MemoryConsolidator` freezes molecules or `MyelinCompiler` compiles reflexes ($E = mc^2$ via `Context.condense()`).
3. **Spacetime Coordinates & The Four Fundamental Forces (`bigbang.physics`)**:
   - `Coord(x, y, scale_depth)`: 3D spatial coordinates across scale hierarchies.
   - *Strong Force*: Organ compartment binding (`_organ.<name>.*` isolation and atomic rollback).
   - *Electromagnetism (`em_force`)*: Affinity matching and polarity attraction/repulsion attenuated by inverse-square spatial distance.
   - *Weak Force*: Controlled transmutation and decay (`declassify`, `ttl`, `MatterStore.decay`, `condense`).
   - *Gravity (`gravitational_mass`, `time_dilation`, `effective_action_cost`)*: Queue backlog + thermal `entropy` + structural mass curve local spacetime, increasing local action cost so peer impulses naturally route toward lighter, cooler Bodies in a Society without a central load balancer.

## Consequences

- `Kernel.physics_summary()` and `bigbang status` expose real physical state (`potential`, `kinetic`, `entropy`, `bound_mass`, `gravitational_mass`, `time_dilation`).
- `bigbang-mesh route` dispatches incoming work across live Incus Bodies using EM affinity + Gravitational curvature.
- Backward-compatible defaults keep all 107 existing M0/M1/ADR-0011/ADR-0012 tests and deterministic replays identical.
